## Delivered boundary

The existing low-resolution FFmpeg contrast/motion/audio-energy extractor and
0.4.1-signal-preview planner now produce a private persisted, revision/full-video-
identity-bound report. Only master/verified zero-drift cameras are analysed.
Unverified/candidate/manual cameras remain excluded and visibly named. One master
at shared zero, <=8 eligible cameras, <=1200s per input and nonnegative event span.
Incomplete signals, gaps and unsupported layouts cannot apply. No guessed sync.
The GUI exposes edit tendency, evidence, excluded cameras, individual source cut
previews, an explicit replacement checkbox, then the normal editor/history.
It does not shorten a video to a target duration or understand scenes/speakers.

Apply rehashes analysed sources, checks the report revision and identities,
replaces video cuts only, and preserves audioClips/audioBuses/sourceRefs exactly.
Existing effects/captions/split layouts and audio truncation are protected by
refusal. Audio is deliberately independent, not automatically camera-following;
users must listen to the finished mix. Original source files remain untouched.
A changed project requires a new analysis, even after Undo/Redo.

Sync and first-cut reuse the same bounded background/snapshot/journal lifecycle.
They exclude edits/exports/each other while running, wait during graceful close,
recover interrupted status, and reject missing/changed persisted reports. Final
status is exposed only after journal persistence. A RED test reproduced both the
missing-report NOT_RUN error and the premature PASS/busy race, then passed GREEN.

The existing extractor now refuses reference-based/remote demuxers (including a
relative-file ffconcat disguised as MP4). A real delayed audio fixture reproduced
first-window energy0.693 before the fix; PTS-preserving resampling now retains
leading silence and the late sound in its actual media-clock window. WebM is
accepted alongside supported standalone video containers.

## Observed defect and measurement

The previous inspector measured sample peak only. A generated stereo ALAC MP4
uses a 48 kHz signal with a right-channel 12 kHz sine, phase pi/4, amplitude 1.2
and gentle 10 ms end fades. Its samples reach 1.2/sqrt(2), below digital full
scale, while the reconstructed sine reaches 1.2: approximately +1.5836 dBTP.
The left channel is a quiet 440 Hz sine. The existing inspector reported
CHECKED_LIMITED despite this known intersample overshoot.

The existing full-output FFmpeg scan now appends `ebur128=peak=true:framelog=verbose`
after the existing silence/sample-peak analysis. FFmpeg's
[filter documentation](https://www.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128) and
[6.1 implementation](https://ffmpeg.org/doxygen/6.1/f__ebur128_8c_source.html)
describe oversampled true-peak metering; its summary is rounded to 0.1 dB.
The report names this method and records dBTP, display precision and coverage.

On the tested FFmpeg, a pulse confined to the final 16 samples was reported as
-4.5 dB without flushing the meter's tail, versus +1.7 dB with 100 ms of analysis
silence. Therefore `apad=pad_dur=0.1` is inserted immediately before the meter,
after existing silence/sample-peak analysis. This affects the null-output
inspection only; it does not append audio to the saved MP4 or extend its duration.

The audio spec's -1.0 dBTP value is a REVIEW reference on the rounded measurement:
values at or above it warn, including a displayed -1.0. It is not a limiter or
quality guarantee. The maximum covers all channels of the first selected output
audio stream and has no peak timestamp. The UI offers whole-output playback;
both peak warnings and unavailable-meter results cannot identify a clip to edit.

`inspection.truePeak` remains a status string; optional `truePeakMeasurement`
holds the method/value/unit/precision. MEASURED, SILENT, UNAVAILABLE, INCOMPLETE
and NOT_APPLICABLE are distinct. Silence uses JSON null, never infinity. Failed
decode/frame completion cannot supply a completed measurement; an absent final
summary cannot reuse an earlier value. Old reports remain unmeasured when opened.
The saved report states `truePeakGuarantee=NOT_GUARANTEED`. No audio correction,
new dependency, provider or external service was added.
